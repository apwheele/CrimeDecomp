Global model decomposition

Observed is the population-weighted monthly sample rate, and sample fitted aggregates the city-specific structural curves using population weights. Global trend and global plus season describe a reference city whose random effects equal zero; they need not match the sample level because cities have different intercepts and the logit transformation is nonlinear.

Seasonal component

Seasonal effect is the change in annualized rate associated with the three-harmonic Fourier curve, relative to the long-run trend.

Shared time period effect

This is the fitted (1 | time_period) effect shared by all cities in a month, transformed to an annualized rate change. It is distinct from the city-by-month effect. The horizontal zero line is the reference.

City detail

Observed is this city's reported rate. Global baseline contains the crime-specific intercept, trend, and season. City fitted additionally contains the city random intercept and the city's trend and seasonal deviations.

City and US-wide trends

The city and US-wide spline trends are centered separately on the logit scale. Centering removes the city intercept so differences between the curves represent differences in long-run trajectory rather than average crime level.

City and US-wide seasonal patterns

The curves compare the city's fitted Fourier seasonal component with the component shared across the RTCI sample. Intercepts, long-run trends, shared time-period effects, and city-month effects are excluded.

City and US-wide residuals

The US-wide residual is the shared (1 | time_period) effect. The city residual adds this city's (1 | cell_id) observation-level effect, matching the residual comparison in the paper.

City-specific trends across the sample

Each grey line is a centered city-specific spline trend; the blue line is the centered global trend. The shaded band contains the middle 80% of city curves at each time point. Hover over a city line to identify it. Centering removes city intercepts so this view compares trend shapes.

City-specific seasonal patterns across the sample

Each grey line is a city's centered Fourier seasonal curve; the blue line is the global seasonal curve. The shaded band contains the middle 80% of city curves. Hover over a city line to identify it. Trend, intercept, and residual terms are excluded.
